Model tournament

Every model played every other model, and a perfect solver player, from each of 1,415 distinct opening positions 0–4 moves deep, once with each side moving first. Models always play their top move, so the openings are what make the games differ.

    Method

    How the tournament was run
    • Openings: every distinct board reachable in 0–4 plies (1 + 7 + 49 + 238 + 1,120 = 1,415). Boards reached by different move orders count once.
    • Each of the 36 pairings played every opening twice, with each player moving first once: 2,830 games per pairing.
    • Adaptive models answer at their own stopping step (cumulative stop probability ≥ 0.5, up to 24 steps). Fixed controls answer at their last step. Illegal moves are masked, so none occurred.
    • The perfect player is Pascal Pons's Connect Four solver with its opening book; among equally good moves it picks the one nearest the centre.
    • Opening values come from the same solver: of the 1,415 openings, for the side to move.
    • Run on both Macs through the Ray queue as eight shards; source is src/cs440_final_project/c4_tournament.py, raw games in results/c4p/tournament/shard-*/games.jsonl.
